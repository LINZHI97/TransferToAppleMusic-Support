# 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

**最后更新日期 / Last Updated：2026-03-18**

TransferToAppleMusic（以下简称"本应用"）由独立开发者开发和运营。我们深知个人信息对您的重要性，并会尽全力保护您的个人信息安全。请您在使用本应用之前，仔细阅读并了解本隐私政策。

TransferToAppleMusic (hereinafter referred to as "the App") is developed and operated by an independent developer. We understand the importance of your personal information and will do our best to protect it. Please read and understand this Privacy Policy before using the App.

---

## 1. 我们收集的信息 / Information We Collect

### 1.1 我们不主动收集个人信息 / We Do Not Actively Collect Personal Information

本应用**不要求用户注册账号**，不收集用户的姓名、电话号码、电子邮件地址或其他个人身份信息。

The App **does not require user registration** and does not collect users' names, phone numbers, email addresses, or other personally identifiable information.

### 1.2 Apple Music 授权 / Apple Music Authorization

本应用使用 Apple MusicKit 框架来访问您的 Apple Music 资料库。当您首次使用时，系统会请求您的授权。我们仅使用此授权来：

- 搜索 Apple Music 曲库中的歌曲
- 将匹配的歌曲添加到您的 Apple Music 资料库

The App uses Apple's MusicKit framework to access your Apple Music library. When you first use the App, the system will request your authorization. We only use this authorization to:

- Search for songs in the Apple Music catalog
- Add matched songs to your Apple Music library

我们**不会**读取、存储或上传您现有的 Apple Music 资料库内容。

We **do not** read, store, or upload the contents of your existing Apple Music library.

### 1.3 歌单信息 / Playlist Information

当您粘贴网易云音乐的分享链接时，本应用会通过该链接解析歌单中的歌曲元数据（歌名、歌手名、专辑名）。这些信息：

- 仅在本地处理，用于在 Apple Music 中搜索匹配
- **不会上传到任何服务器**
- **不会被存储或缓存**
- 使用完毕后即被释放

When you paste a NetEase Cloud Music sharing link, the App parses song metadata (song title, artist name, album name) from the playlist through that link. This information:

- Is processed locally only, used to search for matches in Apple Music
- **Is not uploaded to any server**
- **Is not stored or cached**
- Is released from memory after use

---

## 2. 我们如何使用信息 / How We Use Information

本应用获取的所有信息仅用于以下目的：

All information obtained by the App is used solely for the following purposes:

- **歌单迁移**：解析分享链接中的歌曲信息，在 Apple Music 中搜索匹配，并添加到您的资料库
- **Playlist migration**: Parse song information from sharing links, search for matches in Apple Music, and add them to your library

我们不会将您的信息用于广告、营销、分析或任何其他目的。

We do not use your information for advertising, marketing, analytics, or any other purpose.

## 3. 信息共享与披露 / Information Sharing and Disclosure

我们**不会**与任何第三方共享、出售或披露您的个人信息，除非法律法规要求。

We **do not** share, sell, or disclose your personal information to any third party, unless required by law.

## 4. 第三方服务 / Third-Party Services

### 4.1 Apple MusicKit

本应用使用 Apple MusicKit 框架。您通过 MusicKit 授权的数据受 [Apple 隐私政策](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) 保护。

The App uses Apple's MusicKit framework. Data authorized through MusicKit is protected by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

### 4.2 App 内购买 (IAP)

本应用使用 Apple 的 App 内购买系统。所有支付信息由 Apple 直接处理，我们无法访问您的支付卡号或其他财务信息。

The App uses Apple's In-App Purchase system. All payment information is processed directly by Apple, and we cannot access your payment card number or other financial information.

---

## 5. 数据存储与安全 / Data Storage and Security

- 本应用**不使用自有服务器**
- 所有数据处理在您的设备本地完成
- 转移计数等偏好设置存储在设备本地（UserDefaults）
- 我们不使用任何第三方分析或追踪 SDK

- The App **does not use its own servers**
- All data processing is done locally on your device
- Preference settings such as transfer counts are stored locally on the device (UserDefaults)
- We do not use any third-party analytics or tracking SDKs

The App is not specifically targeted at minors and does not knowingly collect personal information from minors.
